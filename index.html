About Ateneo CODE

📚 What is Organization Development?

Organization Development (OD) is the study of positive planned change in an organization. It focuses on the development of an organization's human resource through carefully planned efforts and interventions, guiding organizations to adapt to changes and continuously improve.

🎯 What CODE Does

As a non-profit, student-run consultancy at Ateneo de Manila University, we provide free tailor-fit Organization Development services for youth organizations. Our self-trained OD consultants reach out to different youth organizations and schools to help them grow and create meaningful impact in their communities.

💡 How We Use OD

OD is a tool that Ateneo CODE uses to pursue its advocacy of empowering youth and youth-oriented organizations. We continuously build on our capabilities as OD consultants through constant research and practice, ensuring that the youth we serve are empowered to initiate and sustain positive change.

🔭 Our Vision

We, the Ateneo Consultants for Organization Development and Empowerment, imbued with Ignatian Values, envision a society of developed individuals by forming sustainable environments conducive for people to flourish. Through the practice of Organization Development (OD), we aim to form the Youth and Youth-Oriented Organizations who will contribute to nation building by initiating positive change within the community.

🚀 Our Mission

Our mission is to empower the youth to build and constantly develop their organizations to engage in nation-building. This is achieved through the sharing of the Organization Development (OD) discipline and implementation of contextualized OD services by our trained consultants.

Engagement Services

2-4 months • 1-3 sessions • Behavioral or Structural Change

Engagement services aim to create behavioral or structural change through carefully planned interventions and training programs.

KSAs (Knowledge, Skills, Attitudes)

Focus: Behavioral changes • Skill-based topics • Develop mindsets and cultures

  • •Leadership Training Seminars
  • •Team Building Sessions
  • •Specific Skills Training (project management, communication)
  • •Speaking Engagements

PSS (Processes, Systems, Structures)

Focus: Structural changes • Documents and organizational frameworks

  • •Vision and Mission Crafting
  • •Constitution Crafting
  • •Organizational Diagnosis
  • •Code of Internal Procedures (CIP) Crafting
  • •Member Development and Retention Programs
  • •Organization Transition

*Other forms of engagements may be explored upon consultation.

Consultation Services

1-2 months • 1-3 sessions • Macro Organizational Problems

Consultation services address macro organizational problems through a dialogic, prescriptive approach.

Recommendations, Not Interventions

  • • Acts as avenues for discourse and conversation regarding the state of your organization
  • • Co-constructed recommendations and plans of action are formed through sessions
  • • More dialogic approach throughout the engagement
  • • Engagement ends once prescriptions are given

Flexible & Tailored

  • • Tailored to meet unique needs and goals of each client
  • • Can address targeted issues
  • • Can provide broader strategic advice

Phases of an Engagement

Our structured approach ensures every engagement is thoughtful and effective.

1

Meet

A representative meets with you to discuss CODE, your organization's background, and possible methods of collaboration.

2

Explore

Discuss your situation and concerns, set time/date/duration of intervention, and sign a contract formalizing our partnership.

3

Plan

A designated team crafts a tailor-fit service, constantly updating you and requesting meetings for proper contextualization.

4

Engage

We deliver the tailor-fitted service for your organization through a series of sessions.

5

Evaluate

A representative meets with you to discuss feedback and points of improvement from the experience.
