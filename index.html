What We Do

📚 What is Organization Development?

Organization Development is the study of positive planned change in an organization, focusing on human resource development through carefully planned efforts and interventions.

🎯 What CODE Does

As a non-profit, student-run consultancy at Ateneo de Manila University, we provide free tailor-fit Organization Development services for youth organizations, helping them grow and create meaningful impact in their communities.
